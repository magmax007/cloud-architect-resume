The Systems Engineer

Technical Lead / Cloud Architect · TRANSACTIS Jul 2020 – NOWONGOINGSchiltigheim

At Transactis, a subsidiary of Société Générale & La Banque Postale, Maxime works within a cross-functional technical department serving Payments, Digital Services & ATM. His remit spans development, architecture, integration and shared platforms, with experience leading developers on initiatives from design through production.

💻 Backend & Payment Engineering

  • Completed work on merchant-side EPI Wero payment services: led developers building approximately 10 Java microservices using hexagonal architecture.
  • Designed merchant and shop onboarding, banking-side API integration, legitimacy checks, transaction/capture processing and EPI API invocation.
  • Owned architecture and implementation through production deployment.

🔗 Distributed Systems & Data

  • Messaging & integration: experience with Kafka, Artemis, IBM MQ and TIBCO EMS, spanning event processing, message brokers and enterprise integration.
  • Data & caching: designed and operated platform services using PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis, with experience in Elasticsearch for search.
  • Event-driven services: designed transaction/capture ingestion and inter-service communication; built webhooks bridging Kafka events and external APIs.
  • Multi-region distribution: designed artifact synchronization through controlled proxies, validating digests before distributing artifacts across internal repositories.

🛡️ Reliability, Performance & Security

  • Designed the payment platform for a 99.9% availability requirement and benchmarked its architecture for millions of transactions.
  • Optimized database access and caching to meet sub-300 ms processing requirements.
  • Personally designed and implemented microservice security with Spring Security and application-level filters; strengthened platform security with service mesh, mTLS and OAuth2.

⚙️ DevOps & Developer Experience

  • Built CI/CD with Git, Jenkins, GitHub Actions and Kubernetes; automated deployments with Ansible and AWX.
  • Led development of Java client-integration tooling and the Transactis developer portal, exposing APIs, documentation, versions, metrics, SLAs and uptime information.
  • Standardized deployment practices across modern and legacy environments.

☁️ Cloud & Platform Engineering

  • Architected and deployed Kubernetes platforms for critical payment services and enterprise workloads.
  • Designed and operated services spanning Kafka, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, networking and traffic management.
  • Built private-cloud capabilities and led platform lifecycle, resiliency and availability initiatives.

👑 Technical Leadership & Architecture

  • Provided technical leadership from a cross-functional department, defining standards, architecture guidelines and modernization roadmaps across Payments, Digital Services and ATM.
  • Drove technology decisions balancing performance, security, reliability and operational needs.
  • Led architecture reviews and workshops; supported teams with technical decisions, mentoring and platform adoption.
